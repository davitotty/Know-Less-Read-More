Models trained on few distinct textbooks answer perfectly on those and near chance on new ones, while models trained on many read new textbooks; a skill left out of training stays at chance, and leaving out the simplest skill also blocks composition; separately trained skills do not combine without training; and applying the same layers a second time extends the chain of rules a model can follow at no cost in parameters. A fourth experiment shows that a symmetry in the training data can keep the basic reading skill from being learned within the budget, and we report what failed.
